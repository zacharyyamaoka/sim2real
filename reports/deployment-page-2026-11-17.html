Decided for you

  • The workbench replaced /deployments/; the card index moved to /deployments/all/ (undo: git revert the commit). site/tests/test_site_flow.py's index checks now point at /deployments/all/ (paths only).
  • Each recording is registered in data_recorder's replay index (~1,040 added; additive) so its link works; registration happens at every site build, once per file.
  • Performance vs fidelity per metric follows sim2real's card families (T2, T3, T7, T8 fidelity; T4, T5, T6, limiter share performance); the repeat floor, data and routines are "evidence".
  • Parts of a servo gate their servo (strict rule); the servo never gates its station (decision 1). Not observable / not run never fails anything.
