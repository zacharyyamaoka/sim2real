The Obsidian frame and the three views (new)

  • The ribbon, matched to Obsidian's geometry (read from like_obsidian_md_viewer's captured obsidian-app.css, never from your running Obsidian): a 44 px strip at the far left; its 40 px header cell holds the sidebar toggle, Obsidian's own icon (the filled bar widens when the sidebar is open); 18 px icons at a 1.75 stroke in 30 × 26 buttons, 4 px apart; the active view on the hover fill, as Obsidian marks its active sidebar tab; tooltips to the right with an arrow. Order: Deployments; Scene · Sheet · Workbench; then Runs in a group of its own. Nothing Obsidian has that we cannot back (settings, help, vault switcher) is drawn.
  • The left sidebar holds the typed component tree in every deployment view. The toggle collapses it and the view takes the room; collapsed or open and its width (drag the edge, double-click resets) are remembered across reloads. On the Deployments list there is no tree, so no sidebar and no toggle.
  • The right inspector (Scene, Sheet) folds from the bar's toggle at the far right (Obsidian's place for the right sidebar's toggle) or Esc; its edge drags. It leads with the picked node's performance, then fidelity, then its parts, every metric round 0 → now, the five questions.
  • Scene (V3): the 3D is the page. Each station's label wears its performance in place (how far up the speed ladder it does its job, its ring coloured by it) and, when it misses, its twin in red; labels never cover each other, a joint, the chips or the ViewCube. A click on a station or a part selects it; a servo pulls apart into driver, motor, gearbox and encoder, each named in place (motor and gearbox: "not observable on this rack"). A time bar moves between round 0 and now.
  • Sheet (V2): the nested table is the page. Performance first, every metric as round 0 · now · change side by side (highest rung tracked, tracking RMS and peak at 0.9, tip stop median, worst stop), fidelity (twin ÷ floor) beside it; the five questions are an optional column group (+ Five questions); What changed dims the rows that did not move. The 3D (only the picked station labelled) and the inspector are docked on the right.
  • Workbench (V1): this morning's tree and node page, unchanged inside.
  • One selection, one round: both live in the URL (n, round), so CAN 11 picked in the Scene is CAN 11 in the Sheet, the Workbench and the tree in Runs, and round 0 stays round 0. Runs sits in the ribbon's second group: reachable from every view; a tree pick there filters the runs to that station, and "Only CAN 11's runs (selected)" does it in one click when you arrive with a selection.

  • Performance leads every page. The rack: all six stations track and stop within the gates up to 0.9 of top speed; 67 of 72 real performance gates (28 as found). The top gap: at full speed, tracking misses on CAN 1, 12, 13, 14, 15 (0.59–1.22° RMS against 0.5°). CAN 11's worst stop is over 1 mm at the 0.4, 0.9 and 1.0 rungs although the median passes. Fidelity sits under it: CAN 11's twin is 2.14× its floor (4.27× as found), the campaign goal's one miss.
  • The goal line drawn: round-by-round charts (rounds 0–8, each station at 0.9 of top speed, "GOOD ≤ 0.5°" and "GOOD ≤ 1 mm"), the speed-ladder plots per station (round 0 dashed under now), and every station × every speed rung.
  • Typed component tree on the left (search, keyboard, collapse; RobotDx's dots), the node's page on the right with tabs: Performance · Metrics (performance, fidelity and evidence as separate groups; round 0 · now · change) · Runs · Five questions · Where it is defined.
  • Table view, now the Sheet (RobotDx's nested table): performance round 0 · now · change, the five question columns as an optional group; Now · As found · What changed; a foldable, resizable inspector whose metric column no longer wraps word by word.
  • Runs view: every play of every scored round (rounds 0–8, 2,233 rows), round 8 by default, a summary on top, filters and sorting; each run's "Open in Rerun" goes through the always-on bam-replay-link launcher on 127.0.0.1:8771 (no more dead 9461/9462 links); a run with no .rrd says "no recording yet". The same table is written as JSON for a later W&B sync.
  • 3D (schematic, labelled "schematic geometry · simulated"; "CAD render" shows the rack's MuJoCo render): a click selects the station, the servo pulls apart into motor, gearbox, encoder and driver.
