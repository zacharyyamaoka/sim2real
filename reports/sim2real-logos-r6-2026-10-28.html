R6-A Clipped Dither is now sim2real's logo and icon; files written, nothing committed, nothing deployed.

Exactly half the gear is pixels: the split is the gear's centre line, and the pixel grid is anchored on it with a cell equal to half a tooth, so the top and bottom teeth are one pixel column thick on the digital side. No pixel is clipped: a block is drawn whole when more than half of it lies inside the gear, and dropped otherwise. The dither ramp is R5-03's.

"For logo do the clipped dither. Make it center tooth only 1 thick though so exactly just half of it is pixels. and then for the clipping don't completely round the pixels if more than 50% of the pixel is showing then draw the entire block. On the quantised silhouette split it evenly as well so the middle tooth is only 1 pixel thick." Zach, 2026-10-28
