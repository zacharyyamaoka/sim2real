Notes

Colour

  • Brand: BAM Grid Purist (Oct 24): ink #0E100E, paper #FBFBF8, one green #2F6F4F.
  • On the ink tile BAM Green is only 3.2:1, too faint for a dashed line, so the twin is drawn in #35DE95: the same hue (OKLCH 159) lifted to L 0.80, 11:1 on ink. The brief said "neon green"; this is the brand green made bright, not the old site lime #C4FF4D.
  • The Paper tile switch shows the other reading: paper tile, ink real, brand green twin, as robotparts' app icon does.

How the marks are built

  • One source, brand/icons/marks.py: each stroke has a role (real, sim, aux) and the 24 px and 256 px files are both generated from it by build.py.
  • Dashes are explicit short paths whose ends sit on the path's own corners (lucide's square-dashed convention), not a stroke-dasharray that can drop a corner.
  • Every mark was rasterised at 16 / 24 / 48 px at 1× by render_check.py and looked at zoomed; five were redrawn after that (Ghost Horn read as lucide share-2, Cast Shadow as user and then search, a mirrored arm as a planet, Twin Tiles touched, Floor Bars was a stock chart).

Wiring it into Clank (not done)

brand/icons/sim2real.iconnode.json is the default in lucide's iconNode shape, so a plugin can do:

const Sim2Real = createLucideIcon('sim2real', iconNode)
ctx.registerRibbonItem({ id: 'sim2real', icon: Sim2Real, tooltip: 'sim2real', side: 'top', command: '…' })

Clank itself was only read, never edited.
