Calibration Routines, graded on the same noise

Every way the house has to calibrate a twin's parameters, as a typed record (type_cards ROUTINES): what it identifies, where it runs, its time and equipment cost, what it needs. The 16 the sysid oracle can run are graded on the same hidden truth at 0, 1x, 3x and 10x their sensor noise (one draw, the same seeds, scaled) and at 1x plus a delay or a quantisation. The bound at 0x is a tenth of the tolerance; at kx it is k tolerances, never wider than not calibrating. Ruler sysid/noise_ladder.json (sha256 4ab3e070da52), committed before any run.

What it means

The reading of these numbers, from the decision record docs/decision-calibration-routines-2026-11-07.md.

  • A model that lacks a term misses by the same amount at every level. The stock quasi-static sweeps: CAN 11's damping 12.5 tolerances off at 0x and at 10x (a negative viscous term, the Stribeck dip their model lacks, fitted under the ki 16000 limit cycle). T10's live re-identification: armature 2.4 tolerances at every level (tanh Coulomb against a Stribeck truth). T10's 3x and 10x passes are the ladder's cap at work: a bound is never wider than not calibrating, and T10 is a little better than the nominal's 2.58.
  • A matched model can still be a biased routine. gear_backlash's UR gravity sweep fits the truth's own gap, yet reads the elbow's 10 arcmin as 12.1 with no noise (1.03 tolerances; shoulder lift 0.33) and grows from there (7.5 at 10x); wrist 1 and wrist 2 are never swept (no pose where gravity beats friction 2.5x: best 0.3x and 2.2x), and the pan and wrist 3 cannot be loaded by gravity at all. All six UR gaps need a reversal or clamp routine; both are recorded, neither is in the oracle. CAN 11's regression likewise carries 0.165 tolerances on gravity (0.33 %) with no noise, so it misses the 0x bar and passes 1x and 3x.
  • Timing beats noise. One late sample costs more than three or ten times the noise: the tracker 10 ms late puts the drum radius at 0.72 tolerances (10x noise: 0.066); the link camera 10 ms late puts CAN 11's gap at 3.4, a fail (3x noise: 0.37); torque 2 ms late puts the UR armature at 0.42 (1x: 0.045). Whole-pixel dot detections fail the line camera's principal point (1.6) at 1x noise. The encoders' real quanta cost nothing measurable (AS5047P through CAN 11's 10:1: 0.203 against 0.195; 14 bit through 9:1 on the UR sweep: 1.233 against 1.232).
  • Every other routine is exact without noise, then grows with it. Within 0.02 tolerances at 0x (the camera and tag fits to 1e-13). The camera and tag fits grow in step with the noise (cell bundle adjustment 0.82, 2.48, 8.41) and sit near one tolerance at 1x (bundle adjustment 0.82, line-scan geometry 0.87), so about 1.2x the committed noise breaks them. The UR dynamics grow faster than the noise (ur5e's worst 0.045, 0.23, 3.4: x5 at 3x, x75 at 10x): the speed noise sits in the regressor's qdd column, and least squares on a noisy regressor is biased by the noise's variance (errors in variables). At 10x those and the cell's camera re-calibration (1.5), the RGBD dot card (5.1) and CAN 11's gap (13.4) fail; the conveyor (0.066, 0.0086), the gripper (8e-4), the UR zero offsets (2.7 and 0.95, inside their 10x bound) and the radiometry (5.2) hold.
  • CAN 11 at 10x raises. The regression's fitted Stribeck curve is steep enough (55.9 N.m.s/rad) that bam_rack_twin refuses the twin (dt x slope / I = 3.8; 1 or more is unstable), so the regression and the gap forecast commissioned from it are failed rows.
  • A drift alarm scaled to the noise floor goes deaf as the noise grows. The gap forecast recovers the wear rate at 1x (0.44 tolerances; crossing forecast at 201.6 h against 197.3 h, 77 h ahead) and 3x (1.58), but the servo card's drift ratio is the link error over the camera's floor, so at 3x the same wear forecasts its crossing at 605 h and never alarms inside the 480 h horizon. A deployed check's sensitivity is then its sensor's noise; a threshold in the gap's own units would not move with it. Not changed here.
