Notes

How they are built

  • Round 3's kit (brand/logos/round3/marks3.py): one 8-tooth gear with a big bore, blocks on a grid, flat steel with a thick rim; the ten marks are in brand/logos/round4/marks4.py.
  • Every mark has the one-colour mode (steel and blocks in ink, knock-outs in the background colour): the silhouette test, and the start of the 24 px icon.
  • Rendered headlessly at 240, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px, light and dark, and looked at. Fixed after looking: Stair Cut and Vertical Drop came out hollow (a keep-mask of grid cells also clipped the tooth tips past the grid, so the cut is now subtracted), and their first staircase was too shallow to see; Size Ramp's steel was clipped to a bracket; Square Holes had too few holes and its squares had flown off the artboard.

Context mocks

  • Clank's sidebar copies the three styles in clank-workbench/reports/media/clank-sidebar-styles-2026-10-26.html (Obsidian rail, Figma rail, Shopify list; light #F6F6F6, dark #262626), with Robot Parts' and BAM Robotics' real icons. Clank itself was only read.
  • The tab strip uses the real 16 and 32 px rasters; its neighbours are robotparts.dev and cad2robot.com's real favicons.
  • Palette: BAM Grid Purist, ink #0E100E, paper #FBFBF8, BAM Green #2F6F4F, lit #35DE95 on dark.

Files

  • brand/logos/round4/NN-slug.svg, -dark.svg, -lockup.svg, -lockup-dark.svg; preview/NN-slug-16.png, -32.png, -mono-16/24/32.png (and dark). R3-04's own files stay in round3/.
  • Rebuild: render4.py --previews, then build4.py (commands in brand/logos/round4/README.md).
